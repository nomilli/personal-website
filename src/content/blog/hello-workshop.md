This is the first post on my new site. I built it as a single home for two
things I care about: a place to keep **build logs** for the projects coming off
my bench, and a spot to **write** about what I'm learning along the way.

## What you'll find here

- **Project logs** — electronics, woodworking, and the occasional odd build.
  Each one documents the process, not just the glamour shot at the end.
- **Blog posts** — notes, tutorials, and longer write-ups.
- **My resume** — if you're here about work, [it's right here](/resume).

## How posts work

Every post is just a Markdown file. I draft in Notion or Google Docs, export to
Markdown, drop in the images, and it shows up here. Images work like this:
