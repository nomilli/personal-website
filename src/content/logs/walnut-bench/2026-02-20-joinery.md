The through-mortises are the heart of this bench. I bored out most of the waste
with a forstner bit, then pared to the knife lines with a chisel. Cut the tenons
slightly long so they'd stand proud of the legs once wedged.

Lesson for next time, already: cut the wedge kerfs in the tenons *before* glue-up.
I nearly assembled the whole thing before remembering.
