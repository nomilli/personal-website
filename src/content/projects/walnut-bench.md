A compact entryway bench in solid black walnut. The brief was simple-looking but
honest: through-tenons, wedged and left proud of the legs, and not a single piece
of hardware in the whole thing.

Three coats of tung oil, sanded to 320 between coats and buffed with a gray pad,
brought the grain to life. The build log below covers how it came together.
